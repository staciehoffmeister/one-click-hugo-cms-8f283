## Values-Based Planning & Stewardship

My experience as a marketing leader and an executive coach taught me that the most valuable measure of wealth isn't net worth, it's having quality human connections. High-net-worth life can be surprisingly isolating. Between caregiving loads and privacy concerns, many women feel they are navigating wealth in a vacuum. Your firm has the opportunity to be the bridge.

Women rely on personal connections to find an advisor at a significantly higher rate than men: 47% vs. 37%.* While both genders care about results, women are looking for bespoke, values-aligned experiences that offer value beyond investment performance.

Community is a growth channel. Whether it's a philanthropy-themed initiative or an heir affinity group, creating a space for shared experience deepens loyalty and generates warm referrals. It turns your practice into a hub rather than just a vendor.
